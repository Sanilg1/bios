# Attribution and independence

This repository was initialized from the NamoID Engineering Challenge template
and responds to a problem statement published at
https://challenges.namoid.in.

The solution is intended to integrate the NamoID identity platform at
https://namoid.in. Platform documentation is available at
https://docs.namoid.in.

The repository and its implementation are owned and maintained by the contributor
identified in `namoid-challenge.json`. NamoID does not automatically review,
endorse, secure, maintain, or warrant community builds.

“NamoID” and associated branding identify the challenge program and source of
the problem statement. They do not transfer ownership of contributor work.
